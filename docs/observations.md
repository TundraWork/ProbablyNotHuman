# What an agent sees

[← Back to the README](../README.md)

**Full-screen panels:** while `flow.modal` is set (the morning daily report, the calendar, Wilde's network, the escape menu, game over, or any item's panel that covers the weighing counter, such as the newspaper, guides and the wanted list), the store is covered just as it is for a player. Panels that leave the counter reachable (a small note, the trade tablet, the phone) do not lock it. Observations then carry only `flow` and `modal` (`id`, the panel's texts and its reachable controls as `id`/`kind`/`label`, or for Wilde's network a compact catalog, and the `actions` that apply), plus `input`/`saves` if requested; the store sections are left out. Actions other than those, `save.*`, `game.quit`, `input.*` and `ui.capture` fail with `modal_open`. The daily report's lines are in the modal's texts and stay in `surfaces.calendar.nightly_report` after closing.

Observation sections: `flow`, `game`, `ui`, `surfaces`, `trade`, `input`, `night`, `saves`; `include` also takes keys of a section (`game.store`, `game.customer`, `surfaces.calendar`). Without `include`, a run returns `game`, `trade`, `surfaces` and `night`, and the main menu returns `saves` and `ui`; ask for `ui` (generic texts and controls for screens without a section) and `input` (raw input bindings) by name. A late-game default compact observation is ~60 KB, almost all of it the item inventories. `compact` also reduces `ui` to plain texts and the controls a click reaches now (id, kind, label and state; ~6 KB instead of ~130 KB). Afterhours inventories appear only while out, item pickers only while shown, `game.bargain` only while bargaining. Compact items keep `id`, `identifier`, `name`, `count`, `types`, `owned`, `value`, `price`, `true_value`, `paid`, `appraisal_value`, `stolen_heat_percent`, `at`, `container`, `water_purity_percent` and `false_features`.

- **Items:** types, base/current/retail/negotiated/true value, every feature with its claimed and real effect, liquid composition and purity, what was paid, the exact appraisal value, stolen goods' heat (the chance security recognises them). `item.inspect` returns the full item with every internal tag.
- **Inventories:** every store and afterhours inventory, open windows and nested containers (open or closed), with role, owner, free cells and cell grids.
- **Store:** cash, rent and due day, favor, retail markup, power, garbage, water purity, locks, readiness flags.
- **Customers:** the one at the counter and today's queue: identity, faction, intent, budget, wanted tags/items, wanted status and report reward, augmentation and whether they can be shot, barter/procurement/gun-order terms.
- **Counter terms** (`trade.counter`): the customer (`customer`: identity, intent, budget, interests and terms, unset fields left out), `weighing_counter_id`, per-item prices and acceptance for both directions, sell total vs. budget, `wanted_stock` (owned items anywhere in the shop this buyer would take, with location), the seller's whole lot on both counters with true values (`buy`), wholesale, and bargain odds (accept / refuse-and-stay / leave, budget cut or raise). `trade.view_mode` names the customer's side (`customer_buys`, `customer_sells`, ...); `trade.confirmation` is the over-budget question `trade.sell_all` can open (`trade.confirm`, or `trade.sell_all {accept_over_budget: true}` to answer yes in the same call).
- **Inventory roles:** `describe.inventory_roles` says what each role is in the shop (player inventory, item containers, the weighing counter as the trading surface with the current customer, the front counter as the customer's side, ...).
- **Difficulty:** `game.store.difficulty` gives the run's difficulty as the new-game screen describes it (`effects`) and the rule values behind it (`rules`, e.g. `landlordGraceOwnedAtStart`); `save.list` describes every difficulty the same way.
- **Reputation:** `game.reputations` gives each faction's standing and its perks (threshold, name, description, active); `trade.counter.your_standing_with_their_faction` gives the standing with the current customer's faction. Action results carry `effects.reputation_change`, and observations `reputation_changes` since the previous observation.
- **Calendar:** active, hidden and future events with price effects, news, the nightly report.
- **Network and phone:** Wilde's network is reached as a player does, by phoning his assistant (`surfaces.phonebook` contact `id` `Wild`) and choosing to browse; while it is open, `modal.upgrades` lists every upgrade with `status` (`buyable`, `unaffordable`, `locked` with `needs`, `cooldown`, `bought`), cost and description, and `network.buy` works. Phonebook with each contact's id, state and cooldown; the phone call's speaker, current line, choices and whether it is the last line.
- **Afterhours:** map, destinations, scavenging attempts and wound odds, health (wounded tonight, wound level); scratch tickets' symbols and prize.
- **Reading:** `surfaces.reading` gives every open reader (newspaper, notes, guides, books, papers) as its text in reading order; `item.activate` on a document returns it too.
- **Notices:** the game's pop-up notifications (scavenging results, wounds, reputation, refusals) come back in `notices`: in an action's result for those it raised, and in an observation for those since the previous one.
- **UI:** texts, controls (including machinery sliders/ranges/progress), visuals and geometry, for anything without a dedicated adapter.
